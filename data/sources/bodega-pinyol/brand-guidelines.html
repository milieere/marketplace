PINYOL
01 · Our story

The corner bodega where the whole street ends up.

Bodega Pinyol is a neighbourhood vermouth bodega in Gràcia known for vegan paella for groups and a dog-friendly terrace. We started in 2019 with vermouth on tap and one big pan on Sundays; the pan got bigger every year.

We welcome guests in Catalan, Spanish and English.

Website: https://bodegapinyol.example

We are

Neighbourly, cheerful, plant-forward, happy to push two tables together.

We are not

A fine-dining room, a tourist trap or a place that rushes you out.

02 · Logo

A wordmark and a stamp

Bodega Pinyol

Primary logo. Bodega Red wordmark. Use it on Paper only.

Bodega Pinyol

Mono logo. Paper wordmark for dark backgrounds: Bodega Red, Oliva or Carbó.

P

Stamp. The round olive-stone stamp, for avatars, stickers and as an ornament.

Keep a clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter "P". Minimum size: at least 96 px wide on screen.

Misuse: don't rotate, recolour or crop the wordmark. Never place the logo on busy photos.

04 · Colour usage

Mostly Paper, a splash of Vermut Red

Balance: 60 % Paper, 30 % Vermut Red and Oliva, 10 % Pinyol Yellow.

Body text is always Carbó on Paper or Paper on Carbó. Headlines may be Paper on Vermut Red.

Text must reach at least 4.5:1 contrast against its background.

Pinyol Yellow

For stickers, stamps and small highlights only.

Never set Pinyol Yellow text on Paper: nobody can read it.

Bodega Pinyol · Brand guidelines · 5
05 · Typography

Bricolage Grotesque and Work Sans

Display and headlines: Bricolage Grotesque

A chunky grotesque sans-serif with character. Weights: Bold 700 and ExtraBold 800.

The paella's big. Bring the whole crew.

Headlines are set in sentence case and are at most 50 characters.

Body: Work Sans

A friendly sans-serif for menus, prices and small print. Weights: Regular 400 and Medium 500.

Layouts are balanced, with small 4 px corners. Photos sit framed beside the text, never full-bleed, and the round Pinyol stamp is our only ornament.

Casual, cheerful, neighbourly.

07 · Photography and our venue

Photography

Natural daylight, big pans shot from above, shared tables, people and dogs on the terrace, candid moments.

Do: overhead pan
Do: terrace crowd
Don't: flash
