01 · Our story

We are the table you share with the people you love.

Casa Brisa is a Mediterranean tapas bar in Barcelona built around sharing plates and long terrace evenings. We opened in El Born in 2014 with one idea: food tastes better when the table is full.

We welcome guests in Spanish, Catalan and English. Our menus, signs and social posts are written in all three.

Website: https://casabrisa.example

We are

Warm, generous, unhurried, rooted in the neighbourhood.

We are not

A place for quick deals, loud promotions or tourist menus.

02 · Logo

One wordmark, three versions

Casa Brisa

Primary logo. Terracotta wordmark. Use it on Sand only.

Casa Brisa

Mono logo. Sand wordmark for dark backgrounds: Terracotta, Olive or Ink.

CB

Icon. For avatars and favicons only.

Keep a clear space around the logo equal to the height of the letter "C". Minimum width: 24 mm in print, 96 px on screen.

Misuse: don't stretch, recolour or outline the wordmark. Never place the logo directly on photos.

Balance: 60 % Sand, 30 % Terracotta and Olive, 10 % Saffron. Body text is always Ink on Sand or Sand on Ink.

Saffron is for small highlights. Never set Saffron text on Sand: it is not readable. Text must reach at least 4.5:1 contrast against its background.

04 · Typography

Fraunces for warmth, Inter for clarity

Display and headlines: Fraunces

A soft, old-style serif. Weights: SemiBold 600 and ExtraBold 800.

Small plates, big conversations.

Headlines are set in sentence case and are at most 60 characters.

Body: Inter

A neutral sans-serif for menus, prices and small print. Weights: Regular 400 and SemiBold 600.

Layouts are airy: generous white space, rounded corners (16 px radius), and a thin Terracotta rule between headline and body.

05 · Tone of voice

Warm, generous, Mediterranean.

We talk like a host, never like an ad. Relaxed but never sloppy: more friendly than formal, lively without shouting.

We say

share · table · together · slow evenings

We don't say

cheap · deal · hurry · limited time

Example headlines

Pull up a chair. The terrace is yours tonight.

Small plates, big conversations.

Made to share, meant to linger.

Never frame an offer as cheap or discounted. We talk about the evening, not the price.

Casa Brisa · Brand guidelines · 6
06 · Photography

Golden hour, full tables

Warm golden-hour light, overhead shots of shared tables, natural textures, candid people.

Photos run full-bleed, edge to edge, with the headline set over the image.

Avoid

Flash photography · empty rooms · close-ups of hands eating
